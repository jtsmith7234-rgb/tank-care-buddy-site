What to include in your message

The more detail you include, the faster we can help. Here's what's most useful when reporting a bug or issue:

  • Device type — e.g. iPhone 14, iPhone SE
  • iOS version — found in Settings → General → About
  • What happened — describe what you did and what went wrong
  • What you expected — what did you think should happen?
  • Screenshots — if the issue is visual, a screenshot helps a lot
  • Steps to reproduce — can you make it happen again? How?

Quick fix tip: If the app is behaving unexpectedly, try a full close and reopen first. Pull up from the bottom of your screen, swipe Tank Care Buddy away, then reopen it. Most minor glitches clear up on a fresh launch.

Response time

We aim to respond to all support emails within 1–2 business days. During busy periods it may take a little longer. We read every message.
