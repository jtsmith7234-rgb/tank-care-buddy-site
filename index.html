Features

Everything your tanks need, in one place.

Built for real fish keepers — from first-timers to serious hobbyists — Tank Care Buddy gives you calm, organized control over your tanks.

Track your tanks

Add as many tanks as you have. Set the name, size, and type — freshwater, planted, shrimp, or goldfish.

Log water changes

Track every water change with gallons, dosing calculations, and notes. Dosing auto-calculates so you don't have to.

Water test history

Log ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, and more. The app color-codes every reading so problem levels stand out instantly.

Browse fish species

73+ species with care profiles — temperature, pH, tank size, compatibility, and sourced references for each one.

Maintenance reminders

Set water change and test reminders. Snooze, skip, or jump straight to the logging form — the reminder resets automatically when you log.

Full history log

Every water change, test, fish addition, and edit is timestamped and saved. Filter by type and expand any entry to see the full details.

Who it's for

Built for fish people at every level.

Whether you just brought home your first betta or you're managing multiple planted setups, Tank Care Buddy keeps things organized without getting in your way.

New fish keepers

The species browser, care guides, and reminders help you avoid the most common beginner mistakes — without needing to Google everything.

Planted tank hobbyists

Track fertilizer dosing alongside water changes, keep detailed test logs, and monitor parameters over time.

Anyone who wants to stay organized

If you've ever forgotten when you last did a water change or what your last nitrate reading was, this app is for you.
