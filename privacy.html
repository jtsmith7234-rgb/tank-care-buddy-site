Privacy Policy

The short version

  • The app collects nothing about you — nothing at all. No name, no email, no account, no location, no device identifier, and no usage tracking.
  • Your tank data never leaves your device. It is written to your phone or tablet and stays there.
  • There is no server. We do not operate a backend, database, or cloud storage of any kind for app data. There is nothing for us to look at, lose, or hand over.
  • The app makes no network requests at all. It works fully offline. Everything it needs — including fonts and all species photos — is bundled inside the app.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. No advertising identifier, no third-party SDK, no cross-app tracking.

This is a deliberate design decision, not a promise about our intentions. The app has no code capable of transmitting your data. See section 1.3.

1.1 Information we collect

From the app: none

Tank Care Buddy does not collect, transmit, receive, sell, share, or store any information about you or your aquariums. Everything you enter — tank names, fish species, water test results, water change logs, equipment records, dosing records, reminder settings, photos, and maintenance notes — is written only to local storage on the device you are using, and is never sent anywhere.

The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address. It does not read your contacts, photos library, location, calendar, health data, or clipboard. It does not generate or transmit a device identifier, advertising identifier, or any other persistent identifier.

We have no ability to access your tank data. We could not produce it if asked, because we never receive it and hold no copy of it.

Notification permissions

If you enable reminders, the app requests permission to send local push notifications. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — no notification data is transmitted to any server. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings › Tank Care Buddy › Notifications.

Usage and analytics data

Tank Care Buddy contains no analytics software of any kind — no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Google Analytics, no advertising SDK, and no custom telemetry. The app does not measure or report what you do inside it.

Separately, and outside of the app, Apple provides developers with aggregate, anonymized App Store statistics such as total downloads and crash counts. This comes from Apple, not from the app, is never linked to an individual person, and we cannot use it to identify you. You can limit even this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Apple may send anonymized crash report data to us through Xcode Organizer and App Store Connect. This data does not include personally identifiable information and is used only to diagnose and fix bugs.

1.2 How we use your information

We do not use your tank or care data in any way, because we never receive it. It remains entirely in your control on your own device.

We do not and will not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with anyone, for any purpose. We do not use your data to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models. There is no circumstance in which app data is disclosed to a third party, because no such data exists on our side.

If you choose to email us for support, we use the contents of your email only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list without your consent and we do not share your address with anyone.

1.3 Data storage and security

All data you enter is stored on your device using localStorage inside the app's private sandbox. iOS enforces that sandbox: no other app on your device can read it, and neither can we. Your data is further protected by your device passcode and Apple's on-device encryption.

We operate no servers, databases, or cloud storage for app data whatsoever. There is no backend to breach, no credentials to steal, and no central copy of anyone's information to expose.

This is enforced technically, not just by policy. The app ships with a Content Security Policy that sets connect-src 'none', which instructs the operating system's web engine to block all outbound network connections from the app — every request, upload, and background transmission. All fonts and species images are bundled inside the app rather than fetched from the internet, so the app functions completely offline and contacts no server, including ours.

The app provides an export and backup feature that saves your data as a file on your device. That file is never transmitted anywhere automatically. You decide where it goes (Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or elsewhere), and once you move it, that copy is governed by whichever service you chose and is your responsibility to manage.

1.4 Third-party services

In the app: none

The app integrates with no third-party service that receives your data. It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or cloud-storage service.

Two Apple services are involved by necessity, and neither gives us your data. The app is distributed through the App Store, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you buy the optional Pro upgrade, Apple processes the payment entirely — we never see or store your name, card, or billing details, and receive only an anonymous confirmation from Apple that a valid purchase exists.

On this website: analytics only

This website (tankcarebuddy.com) is separate from the app and is not quite as private as the app. There is no sign-up form, no login, no comments, and no newsletter — the website never asks you to type anything. In the interest of full transparency, two third parties are involved when you load a page here:

  • Google Analytics 4 runs on the pages of this website, including this one. It records anonymized visit statistics such as page views, approximate region, referring site, and browser type, and it sets cookies to do so. It is used only to understand general website traffic. It is not present in the app and cannot see anything about your aquariums. See Google's Privacy Policy, and note you may block it with any standard browser or content-blocker setting.
  • Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers on this website, which means your IP address is visible to Google when a page loads. Inside the app, these same fonts are bundled locally and no such request is made.

That is the complete list. This website sets no advertising cookies, hosts no ads, and runs no other third-party script. Nothing collected here is ever combined with app data, because no app data exists to combine it with.

The only way to send us personal information is to deliberately email us.

1.5 Data retention

We retain no app data, so there is no retention period on our side and nothing for us to delete. Your data lives on your device until you remove it.

Your data stays on your device until you use "Clear local data" in Settings or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored data permanently — that action cannot be undone by us, since we hold no copy, so export a backup first if you want to keep your history.

Any backup file you exported remains wherever you saved it and is yours to manage. Support emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you, and are deleted on request.

1.6 Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA give you rights over your personal information. Because we never collect it, you already hold every one of those rights directly and can exercise them yourself, instantly, without asking us:

  • Access: All of your data is visible inside the app at any time.
  • Portability / export: Use the export and backup feature in Settings to get a complete copy as a file.
  • Correction: Edit any entry directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Use "Clear local data" in Settings, or delete the app.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing: Nothing to opt out of — we have never sold or shared personal information and do not.
  • Non-discrimination: Every feature you paid for works the same regardless of any privacy choice you make.

If you emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to support@tankcarebuddy.com and we will delete it promptly. That is the only personal information we could ever hold about you.

1.7 Children's privacy

Tank Care Buddy is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it cannot collect personal information from a child. The app contains no ads, no external links to untrusted content, no chat, and no social features. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

1.8 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example to describe a new feature or to improve clarity. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Our commitment: if a future version of Tank Care Buddy ever adds an optional feature that requires sending data off your device — such as syncing between your own devices — it will be clearly described here before release, it will be off unless you deliberately turn it on, and it will never be applied retroactively to data already on your device. We will not quietly begin collecting information that this policy says we do not collect.

1.9 Contact about privacy

Questions about this policy, or about privacy generally, can be sent to support@tankcarebuddy.com. We are based in Huntsville, Alabama, USA. Because we hold no user data, we have not appointed a data protection officer and are not required to.
