Privacy Policy

1.1 Information we collect

Information you provide

Tank Care Buddy is designed to work entirely on your device. All information you enter — tank names, fish species, water test results, water change logs, equipment records, reminder settings, and maintenance notes — is stored locally on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. We do not automatically transmit or receive this data.

Notification permissions

If you enable reminders, the app requests permission to send local push notifications. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — no notification data is transmitted to any server. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings › Tank Care Buddy › Notifications.

Usage and analytics data

Tank Care Buddy does not include any third-party analytics SDK (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar). Apple may provide us with aggregate, anonymized download and crash statistics through App Store Connect as part of standard App Store participation — this data does not include personally identifiable information.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Apple may send anonymized crash report data to us through Xcode Organizer and App Store Connect. This data does not include personally identifiable information and is used only to diagnose and fix bugs.

1.2 How we use your information

Because all tank and care data is stored locally on your device, we do not use it — it remains entirely in your control. If you contact us by email for support or feedback, we use the information in your email only to respond to you and will not share it with third parties.

1.3 Data storage and security

All data you enter is stored on your device using localStorage within the app's private WKWebView sandbox. This storage is protected by iOS's standard app sandboxing. We do not operate servers that store your tank or care data.

The app provides a data export/backup feature that lets you save your data as a file on your device. This file is not sent anywhere automatically — you control where it is stored (Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, etc.).

1.4 Third-party services

Tank Care Buddy does not integrate with third-party services that receive your personal or tank data. The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple's privacy practices govern the App Store experience. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

1.5 Data retention

Your data is retained on your device until you delete the app or use the in-app "Clear local data" option in Settings. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all locally stored data. Any backup files you export remain wherever you saved them and are your responsibility to manage.

1.6 Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our servers, most data-rights requests can be fulfilled entirely by you, on your device:

  • Access: All your data is visible inside the app.
  • Export: Use the export/backup feature in Settings.
  • Delete: Use "Clear local data" in Settings, or delete the app from your device.

If you have contacted us by email and would like that correspondence deleted, email us and we will honor the request promptly.

1.7 Children's privacy

Tank Care Buddy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

1.8 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
